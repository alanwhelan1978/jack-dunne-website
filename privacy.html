Jack Dunne Menswear respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, contact us or make an enquiry, how we use that information, and your rights under data protection law.

Who We Are

Jack Dunne Menswear is an independent menswear retailer based at 79 Main Street, Gorey, Co. Wexford, Ireland.

For the purposes of data protection law, Jack Dunne Menswear is the data controller for personal information collected directly through this website and through our dealings with customers.

Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is necessary to provide our services or respond to you.

When you make an enquiry through our website, particularly in relation to weddings or formal hire, we may collect information such as your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Wedding or event date
  • Number of suits required
  • Any information you choose to include in your message

If you contact us by telephone, email or in person, we may also retain information necessary to deal with your enquiry or provide the service you have requested.

When online gift vouchers become available, we may collect information required to process and fulfil the purchase, including purchaser and recipient names, email addresses, voucher values and personal messages.

Payment card information will be processed securely by our payment provider and will not be stored by Jack Dunne Menswear.

How We Use Your Information

We may use your personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Arrange appointments, fittings and formal hire
  • Communicate with you about a wedding, event or order
  • Process and fulfil gift voucher purchases
  • Provide customer service
  • Maintain appropriate business and transaction records
  • Protect the security and operation of our website
  • Comply with legal, accounting and regulatory requirements

We will not sell your personal information to third parties.

We will not use information submitted through an enquiry form to send you unrelated marketing communications unless you have separately agreed to receive them.

Our Legal Basis for Using Your Information

Under data protection law, we must have a lawful reason for processing personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, we process information because it is necessary to respond to a request you have made, to take steps before entering into or to perform a contract with you, because we have a legitimate business interest in responding to customers and operating our business, or because we are required to comply with a legal obligation.

Where consent is required for a particular use of your information, we will ask for it separately.

Website Enquiries

Our website uses a third-party form service to securely transmit enquiries submitted through the website to Jack Dunne Menswear.

Information submitted through our wedding enquiry form is used for the purpose of responding to your enquiry and assisting with your wedding or formal-hire requirements.

Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive or confidential personal information in the message field.

Gift Vouchers and Payments

When online gift voucher purchasing is introduced, payments will be processed using Stripe, a third-party payment processor.

Stripe will process payment information in accordance with its own privacy and security practices. Jack Dunne Menswear does not need to receive or store your complete payment card details.

We may retain information relating to the voucher purchase, including the purchaser, recipient, voucher value and voucher status, where necessary to issue, administer and redeem the voucher and maintain appropriate transaction records.

Sharing Your Information

We may use trusted service providers to help us operate our website and provide our services.

These may include website hosting, enquiry-form processing, payment processing and other technical service providers.

These providers may process personal information on our behalf only where necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet any legal, accounting or business record-keeping requirements.

Different types of information may need to be retained for different periods. Information that is no longer required will be deleted or securely disposed of where appropriate.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for the website to function correctly.

If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking technologies, we will provide appropriate information and, where required, obtain your consent before those technologies are used.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services.

Jack Dunne Menswear is not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy information provided by those services when you visit them.

Your Data Protection Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation and applicable Irish data protection law, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information in certain circumstances
  • Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Request portability of certain information where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

These rights are subject to certain legal conditions and exceptions.

To exercise your rights or ask a question about how we use your information, contact us at paultdunne@eircom.net or telephone (053) 942 1723.

Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first so that we can try to resolve the matter.

You also have the right to make a complaint to the Data Protection Commission, Ireland's independent data protection authority.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements.

The latest version will always be published on this page with the date of the most recent update.
